# Differences from vanilla

- Battle EXP awards for eligible player Pokémon are multiplied by the selected rate: 1x, 2x, 5x, 10x, or 100x. The default is 1x.
- Adds an EXP. MULT. selector under START > OPTIONS > BATTLE OPTIONS.
- Adds an EXP SHARE selector (OFF, OLD SCHOOL, MODERN, FULL, BALANCE) under the same menu, replacing who receives battle exp and how it's split. The default is OFF (vanilla).
